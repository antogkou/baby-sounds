Safe listening
  • Keep the phone at least 2 metres (about 7 feet) from your baby.
  • Keep the volume low, around 50 dB, about as loud as a quiet shower.
  • Never put the phone in the crib or cot.
  • Use the sleep timer so sound doesn't play all night.
